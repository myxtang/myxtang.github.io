I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC San Diego, advised by [Jorge Poveda](https://poveda.ucsd.edu/), and I also work with [Miroslav Krstic](https://flyingv.ucsd.edu/). I received my B.S. degrees in Electrical Engineering and Applied Math from UC San Diego in 2023. I am grateful to be supported by the [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/).

My research interests are in **control**, **optimization**, **game theory**, **dynamical systems**, and **optimal transport**. My work develops rigorous algorithms for multi-agent decision-making, bandit learning, feedback optimization, and the stability analysis of large-scale systems. Applications include robotics, aerospace systems, cyber-physical systems, and traffic networks.
